# The Monster Card Game II

## Turn

Draw a card, or skip the draw.

Main: use one team action, play one card, or both. A free ability does not spend the team action. A card still uses only one ability unless an effect says it may use another.

Move: withdraw or advance one card.

Energy: attach the energy you gained.

End: statuses tick, then the turn passes.

Skip energy phase means that energy step does not happen.

## Opening and the first turn

Before the first turn, each player may keep or redraw once. The top monster stays. New cards are drawn first, then the replaced cards are shuffled into the deck.

On your first turn you do not draw, and you do not use abilities, effects, or equipment. Play any number of monsters from your hand, and at least one. Each may enter active or withdrawn.

## Cards and energy

A monster has HP, points, and abilities. An effect is played once. Equipment is attached to a monster and has its own HP.

Active is the front of a slot. Withdrawn is the space behind it.

0 is energy the ability requires and does not spend. + is energy the ability spends.

Spare energy is energy on that card which the ability did not use as a 0 or a +.

Your card play is the one card you may play on your turn, the same way a team action is the one ability that spends the turn.

This card means the card using the ability.

Give a card 3 energy. Give two cards 1 energy each. All your monsters gain 1 energy.

## Target and damage

A target is any card you would normally be able to choose: all of your own cards, and some of your opponent's. "20 damage" means 20 damage to a target. "Heal 20 HP" means heal a target.

This card heals 10 HP when the heal is on the card using the ability. Choose another monster to heal 20 HP when it must be a different monster.

Damage types are fire, water, ice, poison, electric, light, sonic, blast, shadow, wind, and earth. Damage with no type does not check weakness. Sonic is piercing.

Weak is ×1.5 damage, rounded to the nearest 10. Extremely weak is twice that damage, rounded to the nearest 10. Only one of those multipliers applies.

Defense lowers an attack by 10 per point, to a minimum of 10. Piercing ignores Defense. The shield on a card is its Defense.

N/6 chance of X succeeds on the highest N faces. A chance that harms you uses the lowest N faces. N/6 chance of X instead replaces the other result. A line that begins with Roll prints every face.

## Leaving play

Slain: a monster at 0 HP is discarded, and its owner loses points equal to that monster. Killed and die mean slain.

Discard: a card in your hand is put into the discard.

Removed: a monster or equipment in play is discarded without being slain. No points are lost.

Destroy: the card is gone from the match. Dark Charge destroys a card in your discard to give that monster 1 energy.

Each player starts at 6 points. You lose at 0 points, or if you have no monsters in play after your first turn.

Bury: put any number of cards from your hand into your deck.

Adjacent: the nearest card on that side, skipping empty slots.

## Statuses

Say the duration when it is not the default. Applying a status that is already there adds the new duration.

Goo, 1 turn. That card cannot use abilities. It may spend 1 energy to remove Goo. Goo a target.

Frozen, 2 turns. That card cannot use abilities. The verb is freeze.

Burn, 3 turns. 20 damage at the end of its owner's turn. 20 fire damage and burn. 20 fire damage and burn for 2 turns.

Poison, 3 turns. 10 damage at the end of its owner's turn, or 30 if that card used an ability or moved. 20 poison damage and poison.

Wet, 3 turns. Water damage against it gains 10 before weakness and Defense.

Weakened, 1 turn. That card's damage is halved, rounded to the nearest 10, before weakness and Defense.

Immobilized is the status Cling gives. That card cannot withdraw or advance. Cling's card deals 10 damage to it at the end of Cling's turn while Cling's card has energy. The immobilized card may spend 1 energy to remove 1 energy from Cling's card.
